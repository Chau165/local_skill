# Retained production craft

Extracted from the archived local animation/core/creative/media guidance, without its exclusive style rules:

- Build one paused finite timeline per composition, register it after construction, avoid unseeded randomness/timers and verify nonsequential seeks.
- Name the viewer takeaway for each timestamped beat. Let motion communicate an actual relation or change; preserve continuity when meaning calls for it. Reading holds must fit the amount of text.
- Treat camera motion as framing. When a shot depicts an interaction, independent actor/target layers and a visible reaction make it readable; charts, typography and talking-head edits do not need invented actors.
- Prepare source-based asset plans and inspect local assets before using them. Keep identifiers and provenance; do not claim a placeholder fulfills a requested finished asset.
- Keep original voice continuous, caption all speech, separate captions from graphic labels, inspect Vietnamese glyphs, safe areas and chart units/baselines.
- Check opening, key action, text hold, ending and every transition at output size. Confirm a real short render and inspect actual frames; passing structural validation alone is insufficient.

The complete original files, script audit gates, 2D cutout blueprints, Three.js adapters and source differences are preserved in the opt-in legacy archive described in this skill's references/legacy-world-motion.md and the repository video-production/manifest.json.
